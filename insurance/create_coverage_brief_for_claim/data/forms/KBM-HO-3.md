# Kestrel Bay Mutual: Homeowners Policy KBM-HO-3 (fictional sample wording)

Policy wording written for this recipe. Kestrel Bay Mutual is a fictional insurer; this is not a
real policy and not legal advice. "We" and "us" mean the insurer; "you" means the named insured.
Each clause starts at a `## <ID> <Type>: <title>` heading.

## COV-01 Coverage: Dwelling (Coverage A)

We cover the dwelling on the residence premises shown in the Declarations, including structures
attached to the dwelling, against direct physical loss caused by a peril insured against in this
policy.

## COV-02 Coverage: Personal property (Coverage C)

We cover personal property owned or used by an insured while it is anywhere in the world against
direct physical loss caused by a peril insured against in this policy. Personal property does
not include animals, motor vehicles or property of roomers and boarders.

## COV-03 Coverage: Fire or lightning

Fire or lightning is a peril insured against. This includes sudden and accidental damage from
smoke caused by a fire on the residence premises.

## COV-04 Coverage: Windstorm or hail

Windstorm or hail is a peril insured against. This peril does not include loss to the interior
of a building caused by rain, snow, sleet, sand or dust unless the direct force of wind or hail
makes an opening in a roof or wall and the rain, snow, sleet, sand or dust enters through this
opening.

## COV-05 Coverage: Accidental discharge or overflow of water

Accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from within a plumbing, heating or air
conditioning system or from within a household appliance is a peril insured against. We also pay
to tear out and replace any part of the building necessary to repair the system or appliance
from which the water escaped.

## COV-06 Coverage: Theft

Theft, including attempted theft and loss of property from a known place when it is likely that
the property has been stolen, is a peril insured against.

## COV-07 Coverage: Falling objects and trees

Falling objects are a peril insured against. We also pay up to $1,000 per loss to remove a tree
that falls on the residence premises because of windstorm or hail and damages a covered
structure.

## COV-08 Coverage: Additional living expense (Coverage D)

If a loss covered under this policy makes the residence premises unfit to live in, we cover any
necessary increase in living expenses you incur so that your household can maintain its normal
standard of living, for the shortest time required to repair the premises.

## EXC-01 Exclusion: Flood and surface water

We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, or overflow of a body of
water such as a river, stream or lake, whether or not driven by wind.

## EXC-02 Exclusion: Water backup from sewers or drains

We do not cover loss caused by water or water-borne material which backs up through sewers or
drains, or which overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump or related equipment.

## EXC-03 Exclusion: Continuous seepage or leakage

We do not cover loss caused by constant or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam over a
period of 14 or more days from within a plumbing, heating or air conditioning system or from
within a household appliance, or the resulting deterioration, wet or dry rot, or mold.

## EXC-04 Exclusion: Wear and tear

We do not cover loss caused by wear and tear, marring or deterioration; mechanical breakdown or
latent defect; rust or other corrosion; or settling, shrinking, bulging or expansion of
pavements, foundations, walls, floors or roofs.

## EXC-05 Exclusion: Earth movement

We do not cover loss caused by earthquake, landslide, mudflow, subsidence, sinkhole or any other
earth sinking, rising or shifting.

## EXC-06 Exclusion: Freezing while unoccupied

We do not cover loss caused by freezing of a plumbing, heating or air conditioning system or of
a household appliance, or by discharge, leakage or overflow from within the system or appliance
caused by freezing, while the dwelling is vacant or unoccupied, unless you have used reasonable
care to maintain heat in the building or to shut off the water supply and drain all systems and
appliances of water.

## EXC-07 Exclusion: Neglect

We do not cover loss caused by neglect, meaning the failure of an insured to use all reasonable
means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss.

## EXC-08 Exclusion: Intentional loss

We do not cover any loss arising out of any act an insured commits or conspires to commit with
the intent to cause a loss.

## CON-01 Condition: Duties after loss

In case of a loss to covered property, you must give prompt notice to us or our agent, protect
the property from further damage, make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect the property,
and keep an accurate record of repair expenses.

## CON-02 Condition: Theft must be reported to the police

In case of theft, you must notify the police and give us the police report number.

## CON-03 Condition: Proof of loss

You must send us, within 60 days after our request, your signed, sworn proof of loss, with an
inventory of damaged personal property showing the quantity, description and amount of loss.

## CON-04 Condition: Deductible

Unless otherwise noted in this policy, we pay only that part of the loss over the deductible
shown in the Declarations.
