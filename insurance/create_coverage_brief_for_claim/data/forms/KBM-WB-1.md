# Kestrel Bay Mutual: Endorsement KBM-WB-1, Water backup and sump discharge (fictional sample wording)

Endorsement wording written for this recipe. It changes Homeowners Policy KBM-HO-3 only where the
policy's Declarations list it. Kestrel Bay Mutual is a fictional insurer; this is not a real
endorsement and not legal advice.

## WB-01 Endorsement: Water backup and sump discharge or overflow

**Replaces:** EXC-02

Exclusion EXC-02 is deleted. We cover direct physical loss caused by water or water-borne material
which backs up through sewers or drains, or which overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump
pump or related equipment, even if the backup or overflow is caused by the failure of the sump
pump. This coverage does not apply to loss caused by flood or surface water, and does not pay to
repair or replace the sump pump or related equipment itself. The most we pay for all loss under
this endorsement in one occurrence is $10,000.
