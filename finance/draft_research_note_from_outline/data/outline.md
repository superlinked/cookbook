# AI accelerators: who supplies them, and what limits supply

## Who designs the chips

- Nvidia's H100 GPU has been one of the most widely used AI chips in data centers.
- Google and Amazon design their own accelerators for their clouds: TPUs at Google, Trainium and
  Inferentia at Amazon.
- We think the big cloud buyers want a second supplier mainly to get better prices from Nvidia.

## Who makes them

- TSMC holds about 70% of the contract chipmaking market and is Nvidia's main supplier.
- SK Hynix, Samsung and Micron are the largest makers of the high bandwidth memory these chips use.
- Chips made at TSMC's plant in Arizona still had to go back to Taiwan for packaging.

## Export controls

- US rules that took effect in October 2022 put Nvidia's data center chips on the export control
  list for China.
- In August 2025, Nvidia and AMD agreed to pay the US government 15% of certain chip sales in China
  to get export licenses.
- Most cloud providers now sign five-year supply contracts for AI chips.

## What we tell clients

- Treat any single supplier's delivery dates as a risk in capacity plans.
- Check how much of each supplier's revenue depends on China before relying on its forecasts.
