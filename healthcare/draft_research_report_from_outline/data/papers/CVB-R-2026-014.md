title: Single-dose study of CVN-114 in humanized PCSK9 mice
---

## Summary

A single subcutaneous dose of CVN-114 at 3 mg/kg lowered serum PCSK9 by 78% at day 14 and LDL
cholesterol by 52% at day 28, compared with saline. The effect lasted: LDL cholesterol was still
41% below saline at day 56.

## Methods

Mice expressing human PCSK9 were fed a Western diet for four weeks before dosing. Groups of eight
received saline or CVN-114 at 1, 3 or 10 mg/kg. Blood was drawn on days 0, 7, 14, 28 and 56.

## Results

At 1 mg/kg, LDL cholesterol fell by 24% at day 28. At 10 mg/kg it fell by 61%, little more than at
3 mg/kg, which suggests the effect levels off above 3 mg/kg. Body weight did not differ between
groups.
