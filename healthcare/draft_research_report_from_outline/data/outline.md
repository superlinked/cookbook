# CVN-114 program update, Q3 2026

## Why PCSK9

- PCSK9 binds the LDL receptor and sends it to be degraded, which raises LDL cholesterol in the
  blood.
- Inclisiran, an siRNA against PCSK9, lowers LDL cholesterol with dosing twice a year.
- Attaching GalNAc to an siRNA delivers it to liver cells.

## What we've shown

- CVN-114 cut PCSK9 mRNA by 87% in human liver cells at 10 nM.
- One 3 mg/kg dose lowered LDL cholesterol by 52% in mice at day 28.
- At 30 mg/kg, liver enzymes rose in some mice; lower doses showed no change.

## Risks

- The drug substance is stable for six months in the fridge.
- A competitor stopped its own PCSK9 siRNA program this year after liver findings.
- We think the dosing interval matters more than any potency gap with inclisiran.

## Next steps

- Start the GLP toxicology study in Q1 2027 at 1 to 10 mg/kg.
- Test monthly and quarterly dosing in the next mouse study.
