# Liver findings with CVN-114 at 30 mg/kg reversed within two weeks

## Why we looked

- GalNAc-siRNA drugs are taken up mainly by liver cells, so the liver is where safety findings
  show up first.
- Some GalNAc-siRNAs cause liver toxicity in rats through off-target effects of the siRNA sequence.
- CVN-114 lowered LDL cholesterol by 52% in mice at 3 mg/kg, so 30 mg/kg is ten times the
  effective dose.

## What we saw

- At 30 mg/kg weekly for four weeks, ALT rose to 2.4 times saline in 3 of 8 mice.
- Their livers showed mild vacuolation and no necrosis.
- ALT was back in the normal range two weeks after the last dose.
- A similar finding in rats led another company to halve its top dose last year.

## What it means

- We think this is a dose effect, not a reason to stop the program.
- The GLP study will keep its top dose at or below 10 mg/kg.
