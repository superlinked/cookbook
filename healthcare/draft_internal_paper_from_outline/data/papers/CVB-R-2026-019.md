title: Stability of the CVN-114 drug substance
---

## Summary

The CVN-114 drug substance was stable for six months at 2 to 8 degrees C, with purity above 98%
at every time point. At 25 degrees C, 3% of the duplex had degraded after three months.

## Methods

Three batches were stored in sealed vials and tested at months 0, 1, 3 and 6 by ion-pair HPLC and
by a duplex melting assay.
