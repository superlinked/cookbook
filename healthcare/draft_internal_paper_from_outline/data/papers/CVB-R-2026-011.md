title: Knockdown of PCSK9 by CVN-114 in primary human hepatocytes
---

## Summary

CVN-114 is our GalNAc-conjugated siRNA against PCSK9. In primary human hepatocytes from three
donors, CVN-114 reduced PCSK9 mRNA by 87% at 10 nM after 72 hours, measured by qPCR against a
non-targeting control siRNA. The IC50 was 0.8 nM, pooled across donors.

## Methods

Hepatocytes were plated on collagen and treated by free uptake, without a transfection reagent, at
eight concentrations from 0.01 to 100 nM. Each concentration was run in triplicate per donor.

## Results

Knockdown was dose dependent in all three donors. Secreted PCSK9 protein in the medium fell by 81%
at 10 nM, measured by ELISA. The non-targeting control siRNA changed neither mRNA nor protein. Cell
viability stayed above 95% at every concentration.
