## Decision

The program review agreed to move CVN-114 into a GLP toxicology study, starting in the first
quarter of 2027, at doses from 1 to 10 mg/kg.

## Open questions

The dosing interval is not settled. The mouse data support dosing every month, and possibly every
three months, but we have not tested repeat dosing beyond four weeks. The team will propose a
design before the GLP protocol is final.
