---

## Summary

Mice received CVN-114 weekly for four weeks at 3, 10 or 30 mg/kg. At 30 mg/kg, ALT rose to 2.4
times the saline group in 3 of 8 animals. At 3 and 10 mg/kg, ALT and AST did not differ from
saline.

## Histology

Livers from the three animals with raised ALT showed mild hepatocyte vacuolation. No necrosis was
seen at any dose. Two weeks after the last dose, ALT in those animals was back within the normal
range.

## Interpretation

The finding appears only at ten times the dose that gives most of the LDL effect. We will watch
liver enzymes closely in the GLP study and keep its top dose at or below 10 mg/kg.
