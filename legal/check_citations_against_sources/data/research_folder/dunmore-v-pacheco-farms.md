# Dunmore v. Pacheco Farms

160 Westm. App. 199 (2011)
Westmark Court of Appeals

## Facts

Dunmore bought seed potatoes from Pacheco Farms under a written supply contract. The potatoes carried a blight that Dunmore discovered two years after delivery. He sued six years and four months after delivery.

## Majority

A cause of action for breach of contract accrues when the breach occurs, not when the injured party discovers it, and the discovery rule does not extend the limitation period for contract claims. The breach occurred on delivery. The claim is time-barred. Affirmed.
