# Westmark limitation of actions, written contracts

Westm. Rev. Code § 4.16.040

## Section (1)

An action upon a contract in writing, or liability express or implied arising out of a written agreement, shall be commenced within six years.

## Section (2)

An action upon an account receivable incurred in the ordinary course of business shall be commenced within six years.

## History

Enacted 1990. Amended 2005 to include contracts under seal.
