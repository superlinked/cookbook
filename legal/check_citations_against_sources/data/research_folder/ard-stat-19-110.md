# Ardena Consumer Protection Act

Ard. Stat. § 19-110

## Section (a)

Unfair or deceptive acts or practices in the conduct of any trade or commerce are unlawful.

## Section (b)

A consumer who suffers an ascertainable loss may recover actual damages, and a court may award up to three times actual damages for a willful violation.

## History

Enacted 2001.
