# Westmark New Home Warranty Act

Westm. Rev. Code § 64.50.040

## Section (1)

A builder of a new single-family residence warrants for two years that the home is free from defects in materials and workmanship.

## Section (2)

The warranty runs to the first purchaser and may not be waived or limited by contract.

## Section (3)

This section does not apply to commercial buildings or to residential buildings of more than four units.

## History

Enacted 2003.
