# Seaview Terrace Homeowners Ass'n v. Lindqvist

195 Westm. App. 260 (2018)
Westmark Court of Appeals

## Facts

The association fined Lindqvist fifty dollars a day for an unapproved shed and sued to collect. It never offered him a hearing.

## Majority

A fine imposed without the notice and opportunity to be heard required by statute is void, and the association may not collect it or record a lien for it. Judgment for Lindqvist.
