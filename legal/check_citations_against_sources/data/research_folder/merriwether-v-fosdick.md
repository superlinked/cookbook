# Merriwether v. Fosdick

149 Westm. App. 205 (2009)
Westmark Court of Appeals

## Facts

Fosdick built a fence six inches over the surveyed line onto Merriwether's lot and refused to move it.

## Majority

A structure that intrudes on the land of another is a continuing trespass, and the owner may obtain an order for its removal. That the intrusion is small does not change the rule. The fence must be moved to the surveyed line. Affirmed.
