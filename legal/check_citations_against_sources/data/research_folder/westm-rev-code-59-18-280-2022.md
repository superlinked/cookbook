# Westmark Residential Landlord-Tenant Act, security deposits

Westm. Rev. Code § 59.18.280 (as amended 2022)

## Section (1)

Within twenty-one days after the termination of the rental agreement and vacation of the premises, the landlord shall give the tenant a full and specific statement of the basis for retaining any of the deposit together with the payment of any refund due the tenant under the terms and conditions of the rental agreement.

## Section (2)

The statement and any refund shall be delivered to the tenant personally or mailed by first-class mail to the tenant's last known address. Proof of mailing is proof of delivery.

## Section (3)

If the landlord fails to give the statement together with any refund due within the time required, the landlord is liable to the tenant for the full amount of the deposit. If the court finds that the landlord intentionally refused to give the statement or refund, the court may award up to two times the amount of the deposit.

## History

Amended 2022. The amendment shortened the period in subsection (1) from forty-five days to twenty-one days and added the double-damages remedy in subsection (3).
