# Ingram v. Vexley Staffing

193 Westm. App. 77 (2018)
Westmark Court of Appeals

## Facts

Vexley withheld Ingram's final paycheck until she returned a company laptop.

## Majority

An employer may not withhold final wages as leverage to recover company property; the remedy is a separate claim for the property. Ingram is entitled to the wages and the statutory penalty. Affirmed.
