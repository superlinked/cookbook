# Briarwood Commons Ass'n v. Tadesse

99 Ard. App. 18 (2022)
Ardena Court of Appeals

## Facts

The association fined Tadesse for flying a sports team banner. He was offered an appeal to the board after the fine was imposed, not a hearing before it.

## Majority

Under Ardena law an association may fine an owner without a prior hearing if its declaration provides for an appeal to the board after the fine is imposed. The fines are upheld.
