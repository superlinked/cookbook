# Ombersley Farms v. Grell Equipment Corp.

96 Ard. App. 140 (2021)
Ardena Court of Appeals

## Facts

A grain dryer Ombersley Farms bought from Grell Equipment overheated and destroyed its own burner assembly. No grain and no other property was damaged.

## Majority

Under Ardena law the economic loss rule bars tort recovery when a defective product damages only itself. The farm's remedy is its warranty claim, which it has pleaded and may pursue. Affirmed.
