# Sandoval v. Northcrest Aggregates

182 Westm. App. 417 (2016)
Westmark Court of Appeals

## Facts

Families living near Northcrest's gravel quarry complained of blasting, crushing noise and dust between ten at night and five in the morning.

## Majority

Noise and dust from lawful industrial activity are a nuisance when they substantially and unreasonably interfere with the use and enjoyment of neighboring land. A quarry may operate, but not all night beside a neighborhood that was there first. The order limiting the quarry's operating hours is affirmed.
