# Ruiz v. Calder Foods, Inc.

181 Westm. App. 12 (2016)
Westmark Court of Appeals

## Facts

Ruiz resigned from Calder Foods with eighty hours of unused vacation. Calder relied on its handbook, which said unused vacation was forfeited on resignation.

## Majority

Because accrued vacation is earned wages, an employer must pay it out when an employee resigns, and a handbook forfeiture provision cannot override the statute. Ruiz is entitled to the vacation pay and to attorney fees. Reversed.
