# Whitcombe v. Arlo

158 Westm. App. 301 (2011)
Westmark Court of Appeals

## Facts

Branches of Arlo's oak grew over the property line and, in a windstorm, damaged Whitcombe's garage roof. Whitcombe sued in trespass and nuisance.

## Majority

Encroaching branches and roots are not a trespass by the tree owner, but the adjoining owner may trim them back to the property line at the adjoining owner's own expense. A tree owner who knows a limb is dead or decayed and lets it hang over a neighbor's building may be liable in nuisance for the damage it causes.

The jury found that Arlo knew the limb was dead. The nuisance award stands; the trespass claim was properly dismissed.
