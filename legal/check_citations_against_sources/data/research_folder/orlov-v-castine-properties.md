# Orlov v. Castine Properties LLC

176 Westm. App. 233 (2015)
Westmark Court of Appeals

## Facts

Castine Properties kept all of Mira Orlov's $1,800 deposit after she moved out. Its statement, sent on time, said only "cleaning and repairs." Orlov sued for the deposit.

## Majority

The statute requires a full and specific statement. A tenant who receives a list of categories with no amounts cannot tell whether any deduction is fair, and cannot contest one without suing. A statement that does not itemize each deduction with its cost is not a full and specific statement, and the landlord forfeits the right to retain any part of the deposit.

Castine argues that the cleaning and repairs were real and cost more than the deposit. That may be so, but it does not matter. The forfeiture follows from the statement, not from the condition of the apartment. Castine may pursue its own claim for damage beyond normal wear in a separate action.

Reversed and remanded for entry of judgment for Orlov for the full deposit.
