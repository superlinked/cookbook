# Quill v. Fenwold Apartments

187 Westm. App. 340 (2017)
Westmark Court of Appeals

## Facts

Quill won a dispute with her landlord over unpaid rent. Her lease awarded attorney fees only to the landlord.

## Majority

A lease clause awarding attorney fees to the landlord is reciprocal by statute, and the prevailing tenant is entitled to reasonable fees. Remanded for an award of fees to Quill.
