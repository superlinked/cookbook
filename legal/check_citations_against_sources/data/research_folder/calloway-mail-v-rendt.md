# Calloway Mail Services v. Rendt

170 Westm. App. 88 (2014)
Westmark Court of Appeals

## Facts

Rendt sent several million unsolicited emails through Calloway's mail servers after Calloway told him to stop.

## Majority

Trespass to chattels requires an intentional interference that impairs the condition, quality or value of the chattel. Calloway showed that its servers slowed and that it bought capacity to cope. That is impairment. Affirmed.
