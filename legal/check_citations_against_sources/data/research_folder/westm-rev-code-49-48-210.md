# Westmark Wage Payment Act, final pay

Westm. Rev. Code § 49.48.210

## Section (1)

When employment ends, all wages due shall be paid at the end of the established pay period in which employment ends.

## Section (2)

Accrued and unused vacation pay is wages and shall be paid to the employee at the end of the established pay period in which employment ends, whether the employee quits or is discharged.

## Section (3)

An agreement or policy that provides for forfeiture of accrued vacation pay on separation is void.

## History

Enacted 2012.
