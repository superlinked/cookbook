# Westmark criminal trespass

Westm. Rev. Code § 9A.52.080

## Section (1)

A person is guilty of criminal trespass in the second degree if he or she knowingly enters or remains unlawfully in or upon the premises of another.

## Section (2)

In this section, premises means any building or any real property that is fenced or otherwise enclosed.

## Section (3)

Criminal trespass in the second degree is a misdemeanor.

## History

Enacted 1995.
