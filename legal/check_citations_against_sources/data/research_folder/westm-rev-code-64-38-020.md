# Westmark Homeowners' Associations Act, fines

Westm. Rev. Code § 64.38.020

## Section (1)

An association may adopt and amend budgets, collect assessments, and enforce its governing documents.

## Section (2)

An association may impose and collect reasonable fines for violations of the governing documents only after giving the owner written notice and an opportunity to be heard before the board.

## Section (3)

Fines must follow a schedule adopted by the board and delivered to every owner.

## History

Enacted 2018.
