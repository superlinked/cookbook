# Delacroix v. Harlan Logistics

92 Ard. App. 305 (2020)
Ardena Court of Appeals

## Facts

Warehouse workers had to pass through security screening after clocking out, which took up to twenty minutes a shift.

## Majority

Time an employee is required to spend on the employer's premises after a shift, including mandatory security screening, is hours worked for overtime purposes under Ardena law. The workers are entitled to unpaid overtime. Affirmed.
