# Ardena Dram Shop Act

Ard. Stat. § 12-301

## Section (a)

A person licensed to sell alcoholic beverages who sells to a visibly intoxicated person is liable for injuries that person causes.

## Section (b)

In this section, licensed seller means a person who holds a retail license issued under chapter 12 and sells alcoholic beverages for consumption on or off the premises.

## History

Enacted 2004.
