# Grayline Retail Partners v. Osterholt Holdings

190 Westm. App. 51 (2019)
Westmark Court of Appeals

## Facts

Grayline leased a storefront from Osterholt for ten years and paid a $60,000 deposit. When the lease ended, Osterholt took seventy days to return the deposit with a list of deductions. Grayline sued under the Residential Landlord-Tenant Act's deposit section.

## Majority

The Residential Landlord-Tenant Act governs dwelling units. A storefront is not one. The Residential Landlord-Tenant Act does not govern deposits under a commercial lease; the lease terms control.

Grayline's lease gave the landlord ninety days to account for the deposit. Osterholt met that deadline. Affirmed.
