# Ardena Residential Tenancies Act, deposits

Ard. Stat. § 33-512

## Section (a)

A landlord shall return the security deposit, less itemized deductions, within thirty days after the tenant surrenders possession.

## Section (b)

Interest accrues on a security deposit held longer than one year, at the rate set each year by the state treasurer.

## Section (c)

A landlord who fails to comply with subsection (a) forfeits the right to make any deduction from the deposit.

## History

Enacted 2016.
