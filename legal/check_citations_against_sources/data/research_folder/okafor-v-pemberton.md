# Okafor v. Pemberton

318 Ard. 77 (2018)
Ardena Supreme Court

## Facts

A seventeen-year-old guest drank beer the Pembertons provided at a graduation party, then drove into a tree. He and his passenger, Okafor, were injured.

## Majority

The legislature has made it a crime to furnish alcohol to a minor. That statute reflects a judgment we may rely on. A social host who knowingly serves alcohol to a person under the legal drinking age is liable for resulting injuries to that person and to third parties.

Our holding is limited to minors. Ludvik v. Ashgrove remains the law for adult guests. Reversed.
