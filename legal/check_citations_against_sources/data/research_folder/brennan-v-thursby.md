# Brennan v. Thursby

209 Westm. 2d 14 (2017)
Westmark Supreme Court

## Facts

Thursby hosted a retirement party at his home. A guest, Lowe, drank steadily for five hours while Thursby refilled his glass. Lowe left at midnight, ran a red light and struck Brennan's car.

## Majority

A host who keeps serving a guest he can see is drunk, and who knows the guest will drive, creates a risk to everyone on the road. A social host who continues to serve alcohol to an adult guest the host knows to be visibly intoxicated owes a duty of care to third parties injured by that guest on the roads.

The duty is narrow. It does not arise from serving drinks alone, and it does not run to the guest. Reversed and remanded for trial.
