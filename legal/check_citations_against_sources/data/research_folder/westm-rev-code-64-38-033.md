# Westmark Homeowners' Associations Act, flag display

Westm. Rev. Code § 64.38.033

## Section (1)

An association may not prohibit the outdoor display of the national flag or the Westmark state flag by an owner on the owner's property.

## Section (2)

An association may adopt reasonable rules on the placement and manner of display, such as the height of a flagpole.

## History

Enacted 2018.
