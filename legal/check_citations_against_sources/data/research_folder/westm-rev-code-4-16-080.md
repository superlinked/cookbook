# Westmark limitation of actions, oral contracts

Westm. Rev. Code § 4.16.080

## Section (1)

An action upon a contract or liability, express or implied, which is not in writing and does not arise out of any written instrument, shall be commenced within three years.

## Section (2)

An action for taking, detaining or injuring personal property shall be commenced within three years.

## History

Enacted 1990.
