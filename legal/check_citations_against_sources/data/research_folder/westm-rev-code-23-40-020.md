# Westmark Noncompetition Covenants Act

Westm. Rev. Code § 23.40.020 (2021)

## Section (1)

This section applies to noncompetition covenants entered into on or after January 1, 2021. A noncompetition covenant is void and unenforceable against an employee whose annual earnings are below the threshold set under subsection (3).

## Section (2)

A noncompetition covenant that exceeds eighteen months after termination of employment is presumed unreasonable and unenforceable. A party seeking enforcement may rebut the presumption only by proving by clear and convincing evidence that a duration longer than eighteen months is necessary to protect the party's business or goodwill.

## Section (3)

The earnings threshold is one hundred thousand dollars for 2021. Beginning in 2022, the director of labor and industries shall adjust the threshold each year for inflation and publish the adjusted threshold by September 30 of the preceding year.

## Section (4)

If a court or arbitrator determines that a noncompetition covenant violates this section, the violator must pay the aggrieved person the greater of actual damages or a statutory penalty of five thousand dollars, plus reasonable attorneys' fees, expenses and costs incurred in the proceeding.

## History

Enacted 2020, effective January 1, 2021.
